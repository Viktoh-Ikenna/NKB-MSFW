# Nailangs Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 5 November 2026_

Nailangs is an on-screen keyboard for typing the special letters and tone marks
of Igbo, Hausa, Yorùbá and other Nigerian languages on Windows, published by
Nailangs Nigeria Limited. This policy explains what the app does with your
information.

## Summary

**Nailangs does not collect, store, or share any personal data.** It has no
accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and does not connect to the internet.

## Keyboard and mouse input

While the **pop-out keyboard** is open, Nailangs watches key presses and mouse
clicks across Windows. It needs to do this to:

- turn the physical keys you press into Nailangs letters and tone marks, and
- know when to stop waiting for a tone mark (for example, when you click
  elsewhere or press Space, Backspace, Enter or an arrow key).

This input is handled **in memory, on your device, at the moment you type**.
Nailangs keeps at most the last letter you typed, so it can add a tone mark to
it, and forgets it as soon as you move on. Keystrokes are never written to disk,
logged, or sent anywhere. When you close the pop-out keyboard, Nailangs stops
watching input completely.

The letters you type with Nailangs go only to the app you are typing in, the
same way an ordinary keyboard works.

## Data we collect

None. Nailangs does not collect or transmit:

- what you type,
- your name, email address or any other contact details,
- your location, device identifiers, or usage statistics,
- crash reports.

## Data stored on your device

Nailangs does not save files, settings or history on your device.

## Internet access

Nailangs does not use the internet. It works entirely offline.

## Children

Nailangs collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Third parties

Nailangs contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking code, and
shares no data with anyone. If you download Nailangs from the Microsoft Store,
Microsoft's own privacy statement covers the Store itself
(https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).
